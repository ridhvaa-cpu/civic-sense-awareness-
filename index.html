01 — UNDERSTAND

What does civic sense really mean?

Civic sense means being aware of our duties as members of a community and behaving in ways that respect other people, public property and the environment.

It shows in everyday choices: using a dustbin, following traffic rules, keeping noise levels considerate and treating everyone with respect. A better community is built when each of us does our part.
